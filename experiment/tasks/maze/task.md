# maze

Standard input is a rectangular maze: `#` is a wall, `.` is open, `S` is the start and `E` is the
exit (exactly one of each, both open cells). You move one step at a time up, down, left or right,
never into a wall or outside the grid. Print the minimum number of steps from S to E, or `-1` if E
cannot be reached. On a second line print the number of open cells (including S and E) reachable
from S.
